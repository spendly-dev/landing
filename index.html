Questions, answered.

Is Moongen free?

Yes. Moongen is free to download and has no subscriptions in the current version. A few features, such as receipt scanning, have monthly usage limits, which you can see in the app.

Does Moongen connect to my bank?

No. You add transactions yourself, by typing, scanning a receipt or asking the assistant. Moongen never asks for bank logins.

Can I track several currencies?

Yes. Each wallet has its own currency, and you can move money between wallets with transfers.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. Start in guest mode and create an account later with Apple, Google or email.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Export your transactions to CSV at any time.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet as an app. On Android or a computer, you can use Moongen in the browser at moongen.app.
